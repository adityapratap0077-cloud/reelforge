Privacy

ReelForge is a promo-reel service run by Aditya Pratap. This page explains, in plain terms, what information the service handles.

What we collect

This website itself has no account system and no order form. Orders happen over Instagram DM. When you place an order, you send us the details we need to make your reel: things like your business name, how to contact you, and brand assets such as your logo, photos or product details. We only receive what you choose to send in that conversation.

How it is used

What you send is used to produce and deliver your reel, and to reply to you about your order. We do not sell your information to anyone.

Contact

Questions about your information, or want something you sent us deleted? Email adityapratap0077@gmail.com.
